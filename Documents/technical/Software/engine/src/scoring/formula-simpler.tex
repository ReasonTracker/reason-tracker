\section*{Reason Tracker scoring formula (simplified)}

Assumptions: every claim is reversible, and the map has no cycles.

Parent score:
\begin{equation*}
\mathrm{score}_n = \frac{\sum_c \mathrm{isPro}_c \cdot \mathrm{score}_c \cdot \mathrm{weight}_c}{\sum_c \mathrm{weight}_c}.
\end{equation*}

Weight of one claim:
\begin{equation*}
\mathrm{weight}_c = \rho_c \cdot |\mathrm{score}_c|.
\end{equation*}

Relevance of one edge:
\begin{equation*}
\rho = \frac{\prod_{\mathrm{isPro}_r \cdot \mathrm{score}_r > 0} (1 + |\mathrm{score}_r|)}{\prod_{\mathrm{isPro}_r \cdot \mathrm{score}_r < 0} (1 + |\mathrm{score}_r|)}.
\end{equation*}

Base case: a claim with no child claims scores $1$.

Definitions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $n$: the parent claim.
  \item $c$: a claim.
  \item $\mathrm{isPro}_c$: $+1$ if $c$ is pro $n$, $-1$ if $c$ is con.
  \item $\mathrm{score}$: score of a claim (subscript says which claim).
  \item $\mathrm{weight}_c$: weight of claim $c$ (relevance $\times$
        how much it stands).
  \item $\rho_c$: relevance multiplier of claim $c$'s edge ($\rho$ in
        the relevance section is this, for one edge).
  \item $r$: a relevance claim on an edge.
  \item $\mathrm{isPro}_r$: $+1$ if $r$ is pro importance, $-1$ if $r$
        is con.
\end{itemize}
